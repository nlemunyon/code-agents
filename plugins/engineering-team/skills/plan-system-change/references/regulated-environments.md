# Regulated-environment planning

## AWS GovCloud

- Resolve the `aws-us-gov` Region, account, credentials, ARNs, endpoints, FIPS
  requirements, service availability, and cross-partition flows.
- Cover IAM, KMS, secrets, network topology, logging, backup, recovery,
  infrastructure validation, promotion, rollback, and shared responsibility.
- Verify material claims against current primary AWS documentation for the
  selected Region and partition.

## Standards and assurance

- Resolve the applicable standard, profile, baseline, version, and accountable
  owner before mapping controls.
- Cite exact control identifiers and distinguish guidance, implementation,
  objective evidence, assessment, compensating controls, and residual risk.
- Never claim compliance, certification, or authorization from engineering
  review alone.
