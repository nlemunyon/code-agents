# Documentation planning

- Inventory every affected public surface and link its source, consumers, docs,
  owner, and verification.
- Cover installation, configuration, usage, architecture, extension points,
  tests, APIs, clients, MCP, schemas, errors, compatibility, and operations.
- Include release, upgrade, migration, deprecation, rollback, and recovery
  guidance when applicable.
- Require verified usage, expected results, and caller-facing error meaning and
  recovery, or an explicit `Not applicable` reason.
- Check meaningful authored sections at U.S. grade 12 or lower.
- Require portable paths, current links, executable examples, and generated-doc
  checks.
- Fail closed for a missing inventory, owner, check, example, expected result,
  or error guidance.
