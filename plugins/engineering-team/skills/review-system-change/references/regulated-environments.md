# Regulated-environment review

- For GovCloud, verify `aws-us-gov` Regions, endpoints, ARNs, service
  availability, FIPS behavior, IAM, networking, encryption, logging, backup,
  recovery, and cross-partition flows against current primary documentation.
- Derive standards, profiles, baselines, versions, and owners from project
  evidence; cite exact controls.
- Separate implementation, evidence, assessment, compensating controls, and
  residual risk. Never infer compliance, certification, or authorization.
