# Security-related documentation audit

- Inventory public documentation affected by findings with source, owner, and
  verification.
- Require verified safe usage, expected results, and caller error meaning and
  recovery, or an explicit `Not applicable` reason.
- Enforce U.S. grade 12 or lower prose, portable paths, and the fail-closed
  documentation-quality gate.
- Keep remediation guidance distinct from controls, evidence, risk acceptance,
  compliance, and certification.
